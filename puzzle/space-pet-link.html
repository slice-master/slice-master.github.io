Space Pet Link - Sci-Fi Cosmic Alien Tile Connect

Match cute cosmic creatures in Space Pet Link! Connect matching alien pets across starfield puzzle grids using connecting paths that turn no more than two 90-degree corners.

Key Features:

  • Adorable Sci-Fi Pets: Match cute space kittens, one-eyed alien puppies, and star bunnies.
  • Cosmic Puzzle Challenge: Clear starfield boards before the oxygen timer bar reaches zero.
  • Bomb & Freeze Power-Ups: Use cosmic laser bombs to clear stubborn tiles or freeze the timer bar.
  • Multiple Level Galaxies: Progress through dozens of galaxy stages with shifting grid shapes.

How to Play:

  • Select First Pet: Left-click on an alien pet tile on the board.
  • Select Matching Pet: Left-click an identical pet tile with a clear connecting path.
  • Clear Board: Match all alien pet pairs before the timer at the top runs out.
