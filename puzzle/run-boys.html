Run Boys - Multiplayer Ragdoll Obstacle Knockout

Run Boys is a hilarious multiplayer knockout race! Dash, jump, dive, and punch your way through chaotic obstacle courses alongside dozens of ragdoll opponents. Avoid swinging hammers and don't fall off!

Key Features:

  • Wacky Ragdoll Knockout Racing: Race past giant swinging axes, popping doors, and spinning beams.
  • Multiplayer Competitions: Compete in multi-round elimination matches until one champion remains.
  • Hilarious Character Skins: Customize your runner with funny animal costumes, superhero outfits, and hats.
  • Dynamic Physics Maps: Master slippery ice slides, bouncing trampoline pads, and see-saw platforms.

How to Play:

  • Move Character: Use WASD or Arrow Keys to run left, right, forward, and backward.
  • Jump / Vault: Press Spacebar to hop over low barriers and rolling logs.
  • Dive Forward: Press Left Shift or Right Mouse Click while in mid-air to dive across finish lines.
