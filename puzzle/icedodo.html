Ice Dodo

High-Speed 3D Icy Platform Rush

Ice Dodo is a thrilling 3D obstacle avoidance game. Take control of a courageous frozen dodo bird sliding at breakneck speeds across floating geometric courses. Dodge pillars, jump over gaps, and drift around tight turns to reach the finish line!

Key Features:

  • Fast-Paced 3D Action: High-speed momentum with precise steering.
  • Creative Level Design: Hundreds of challenging user and developer-crafted maps.
  • Vibrant Geometric Worlds: Neon aesthetics and pulse-pounding rhythm.

How to Play:

  • Steer Left/Right: A/D or Left/Right Arrow Keys
  • Jump / Hop: Spacebar or W / Up Arrow
