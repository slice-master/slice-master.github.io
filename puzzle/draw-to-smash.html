Draw To Smash! - Creative Logic Drawing Puzzle

Exercise your brain in Draw To Smash! Draw custom lines, doodles, and heavy shapes into the air and let gravity drop them down to smash all the angry green eggs scattered across each level.

Key Features:

  • Physics Drawing Freedom: Draw any line, box, spike, or doodle shape to solve puzzles.
  • Hundreds of Logic Levels: Encounter moving platforms, portals, ropes, and bouncing obstacles.
  • Three-Star Rating: Solve levels using fewer drawn strokes to earn 3-star perfection.
  • Hilarious Egg Reactions: Enjoy funny sound effects and animations as eggs smash.

How to Play:

  • Draw Object: Click and drag Left Mouse Button across the canvas screen to draw a custom shape.
  • Release to Drop: Let go of the mouse button to spawn the shape and let gravity take effect.
  • Reset Level: Click the Retry button if your drawn shape misses target eggs.
