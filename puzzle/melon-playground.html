Melon Playground

Ultimate Open-Ended Ragdoll Sandbox

Melon Playground is a creative physics sandbox simulator inspired by People Playground. Spawn ragdoll characters like Melon, Pumpkin, and Corn, equip them with melee weapons, firearms, rocket launchers, and syringes, and create wild physical experiments in zero rules!

Key Features:

  • Endless Creative Freedom: Spawn ragdolls, contraptions, and weapons without limits.
  • Realistic Physics and Damage: Destructible body parts, fluids, and combustion physics.
  • Huge Toybox Arsenal: Guns, swords, syringes, landmines, tanks, and physics thrusters.

How to Play:

  • Spawn Items: Select from left side menu
  • Interact / Drag: Left Click and Drag
  • Freeze / Slow Motion: Spacebar or Pause Button
  • Context Menu: Right Click on objects
