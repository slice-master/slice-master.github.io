Wordle Unlimited

Guess the Secret Word with Unlimited Plays

Wordle Unlimited takes the world's most viral word deduction game and removes the once-a-day limit! You have 6 attempts to guess a secret 5-letter word. Green tiles indicate the right letter in the right spot, yellow means right letter wrong spot, and gray means not in the word.

Key Features:

  • Play as Much as You Want: Endless new random word puzzles without daily locks.
  • Color-Coded Feedback: Immediate visual clues guide your linguistic deductions.
  • Vocabulary Builder: Expand your word bank and track win rate statistics.

How to Play:

  • Type Letters: Keyboard A-Z or Click on-screen keyboard
  • Submit Guess: Enter Key
  • Delete Letter: Backspace
