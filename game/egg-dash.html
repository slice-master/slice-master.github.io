Egg Dash - Rhythm Geometry Jumping Dash

Egg Dash delivers high-speed rhythm platforming! Guide a fragile egg sliding through geometric obstacle courses filled with sharp spikes, moving blocks, and jump pads in sync with energetic music.

Key Features:

  • Rhythmic Geometry Gameplay: Jump in time with energetic soundtrack beats to survive.
  • Frantic Reflex Action: Test your reaction speed against instant-death spike traps and walls.
  • Customizable Egg Skins: Unlock cool egg characters, glowing trails, and fun hats.
  • Practice Mode: Place checkpoints to master difficult level sections before official runs.

How to Play:

  • Jump: Press Spacebar, Up Arrow, W, or Left Mouse Click to hop over spikes and obstacles.
  • Hold to Multi-Jump: Hold down the jump key to continuously hop across series of pads.
  • Restart Level: Instant restart upon crashing to keep momentum high.
