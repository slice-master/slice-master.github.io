Vex X3M 2 - Nitro Boost Stunt Bike Trials

Vex X3M 2 returns with explosive nitro stunts! Accelerate through loop-de-loops, detonate TNT ramps, and blast past red laser barriers on upgraded motocross bikes across 30 new levels.

Key Features:

  • Explosive Nitro Boosts: Hit nitro pads and detonate TNT crates to launch across massive gaps.
  • Insane Flip Stunts: Land backflips and 360 air flips to shave seconds off your race timer.
  • Deadly Trap Elements: Navigate moving saw platforms, collapsing bridges, and spike wheels.
  • Custom Rider Gear: Unlock neon bikes, flaming wheels, and futuristic racing helmets.

How to Play:

  • Gas / Brake: W / Up Arrow to accelerate; S / Down Arrow to brake.
  • Air Balance: A/D or Left/Right Arrow keys to lean back/forward and perform flips.
  • Nitro Boost: Press Left Shift or Spacebar when nitro gauge is filled.
