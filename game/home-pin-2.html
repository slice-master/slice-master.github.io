Home Pin 2 - Pin-Pulling Family Rescue Puzzle

Home Pin 2 is a heartwarming pin-pulling puzzle story! Help a mother and her young child restore a secret manor by pulling iron pins in the correct order to collect gold, warmth, and supplies while avoiding hazards.

Key Features:

  • Engaging Pin Logic Puzzles: Solve mechanical pin puzzles to deliver fire, food, and coins.
  • Manor Renovation: Use puzzle rewards to repair rooms, buy furniture, and decorate the mansion.
  • Hazard Avoidance: Block wild ice blasts, bomb explosives, and thieves by dropping heavy weights.
  • Charming Storyline: Follow an emotional story of home restoration and family care.

How to Play:

  • Analyze Puzzle: Look at the pins holding back gold, fire, ice, or bombs.
  • Pull Pin: Left-click or tap on an iron pin to pull it out and trigger object drops.
  • Clear Level: Safely drop fire to warm the room and collect gold without letting bombs hit family.
