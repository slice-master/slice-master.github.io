Hill Climb Pixel Car - Retro Pixel Physics Hill Racer

Conquer steep mountain slopes in Hill Climb Pixel Car! Drive retro pixel-art cars over bumpy hills, bridge gaps, collect fuel cans, and flip across finish lines without flipping upside down.

Key Features:

  • Charming Pixel Art Style: Retro 8-bit vehicle designs, mountain landscapes, and funny crash physics.
  • Physics Balance Gameplay: Balance gas and brake pedals carefully to avoid backflipping.
  • Vehicle Customization: Unlock pixel jeeps, monster trucks, buses, and retro sports cars.
  • Endless Mountain Tracks: Drive as far as possible while collecting coin caches and gas canisters.

How to Play:

  • Accelerate / Lean Back: Press D or Right Arrow key to gas forward and tilt nose up.
  • Brake / Lean Forward: Press A or Left Arrow key to brake/reverse and tilt nose down.
  • Maintain Balance: Feather gas and brake controls in mid-air to land smoothly on wheels.
