Car Speed Racing Tycoon - Motorsport Empire & Racing Simulation

Build and expand your racing team empire in Car Speed Racing Tycoon! Purchase high-performance supercars, upgrade garage facilities, hire top pit crews, and race to dominate global championships.

Key Features:

  • Deep Tycoon Management: Manage garage finances, vehicle repairs, and tech upgrades.
  • High-Octane Track Racing: Take direct wheel control during intense tournament finals.
  • Vehicle Customization: Customize spoilers, engine turbos, rim designs, and neon wraps.
  • Global Championship Cups: Progress through amateur leagues up to world grand prix races.

How to Play:

  • Manage Garage: Use the Mouse to select upgrades, buy new cars, and hire mechanics.
  • Race Control: Use WASD or Arrow keys to steer and drive your car during live race events.
  • Activate Nitro: Press Spacebar or Shift to trigger nitrous boost during straightaway passes.
