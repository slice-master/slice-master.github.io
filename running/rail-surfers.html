Rail Surfers - Subway Track Hoverboard Parkour Dash

Rail Surfers puts you on the runaway railway lines! Grind along electrical rails, surf on high-speed hoverboards, dodge oncoming commuter trains, and snatch glittering gold coins on the run.

Key Features:

  • Multi-Track Action: Seamlessly hop across 3 high-speed railway lanes.
  • Hoverboard Surf Mode: Double-tap to deploy crash-proof boards with unique perks.
  • Jetpacks & Coin Magnets: Soar above trains to collect sky coins in complete safety.
  • Upgradable Power-Ups: Increase boost durations and score multipliers in the shop.

How to Play:

  • Left / Right Arrow or A/D: Switch railway lanes.
  • Up Arrow / W: Jump over barriers and train bumpers.
  • Down Arrow / S: Roll and slide under signal poles.
  • Spacebar: Activate hoverboard.
  • Pro Tip: Use hoverboards whenever trains block all three lanes for emergency crash insurance.
