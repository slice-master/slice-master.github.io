Subway Surfers: New York City - World Tour Through the Concrete Jungle

Subway Surfers heads to the Big Apple! Grind the rails of the iconic New York City subway system, weave through yellow taxis and subway cars, and show off your graffiti skills in the bustling metropolis.

Key Features:

  • Iconic NYC Landmarks: Dash past Statue of Liberty billboards, subway stations, and Broadway lights.
  • Tony the Street Kid: Unlock Tony with his sporty tracksuits and classic skateboards.
  • High-Flying Power-Ups: Grab the pogo stick, jetpack, and coin magnets to maximize score.
  • Super Responsive 3-Lane Controls: Seamless jumps, slides, and lightning-fast lane changes.

How to Play:

  • Left / Right Arrow: Switch between subway tracks.
  • Up Arrow: Leap onto train roofs and over roadblocks.
  • Down Arrow: Slide under turnstiles and low obstacles.
  • Spacebar: Ride your hoverboard.
  • Pro Tip: Stay on train rooftops whenever possible—they are safer and pack more gold coins.
