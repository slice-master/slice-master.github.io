Snow Rider Obby Parkour - Sledding Obby Platformer on Snowy Sky Tracks

Snow Rider Obby Parkour merges the speed of downhill sledding with the precision of sky-high obby courses! Guide your sleigh across floating ice platforms, dodge swinging icicles, and beat the checkpoint clock.

Key Features:

  • Hybrid Sled-Platformer Mechanics: Carry sliding momentum across tricky parkour platforms.
  • Perilous Sky Courses: Race across floating ice blocks, slippery loops, and jump pads.
  • Checkpoint System: Respawn smoothly without redoing the entire run.
  • Colorful Winter Vistas: Vibrant snowy landscapes suspended high in the clouds.

How to Play:

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Steer and balance your sled.
  • Spacebar: Jump over voids and rotating obstacle bars.
  • Pro Tip: Tap the brake when landing on ice pads to avoid skidding over the edge.
