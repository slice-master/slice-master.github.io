Embark on the Epic Snail Bob Adventure Series

Join the courageous and charming gastropod hero in the complete Snail Bob adventure franchise on Slice Master! Guide Bob safely through complex mechanical obstacle courses, lush jungles, mysterious space stations, ancient Egyptian tombs, and fantasy realms.

Bob never stops crawling forward, so it is up to your quick thinking to manipulate levers, activate conveyor belts, rotate bridges, launch springs, and hide Bob inside his shell before danger strikes. Don't forget to spot all three hidden stars in every level to achieve three-star mastery!

With humorous storylines, delightful animations, and brain-teasing physics puzzles, Snail Bob offers a heartwarming, family-friendly adventure suitable for puzzle enthusiasts of all ages.
