Snail Bob 6: Winter Story - Cozy Winter Christmas Rescue

Save Christmas in Snail Bob 6: Winter Story! Help Bob navigate snowy landscapes, icy slopes, and Christmas puzzle contraptions to rescue Santa Snail from the villainous Mr. Green.

Key Features:

  • Festive Winter Theme: Snowy levels, Christmas trees, wrapped presents, and holiday tunes.
  • Ice & Snow Mechanics: Slide fast across frozen ice patches, clear snowbanks, and use heaters.
  • Holiday Puzzles: Operate toy cannons, ice crushers, and Christmas tree lights.
  • Secret Stars: Spot 3 hidden stars buried under snow and ornaments in each stage.

How to Play:

  • Pause / Hide: Click Bob or press Spacebar to stop crawling and duck into shell.
  • Turn / Fast: Press 1 key to reverse direction; press 2 key to toggle fast speed.
  • Winter Tools: Left-click heaters, ice levers, and gift boxes to solve puzzles.
