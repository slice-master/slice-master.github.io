Tennis Open

Grand Slam Tennis Tournament Action

Tennis Open puts you at center court of world-renowned Grand Slam championships! Master heavy topspin, delicate slices, aggressive volleys, and 120mph serves as you battle through prestigious tournaments in Australia, France, Britain, and the USA.

Key Features:

  • Multiple Court Surfaces: Grass, clay, and hard courts alter ball speed and bounce.
  • Comprehensive Shot Selection: Flat drives, topspin lobs, drop shots, and aces.
  • Championship Career Mode: Train your player stats and rise to World No. 1.

How to Play:

  • Move Player: WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Normal Shot: J or Z
  • Slice / Lob: K or X
  • Serve: Spacebar
