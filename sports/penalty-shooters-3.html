Next-Level Shootouts in Penalty Shooters 3

Penalty Shooters 3 takes the beloved penalty kick series to new heights. Choose from hundreds of club teams across 6 tiered leagues. Level up your kicker power and goalkeeper reflexes, earn sponsor gems, and dominate high-stakes tournament penalty shootouts!

Key Features:

  • 6 Tiered Leagues: Progress from regional leagues to the World Tier.
  • Player Upgrades: Boost shot power, accuracy, and goalkeeper agility.
  • Crisp Modern Animations: Fluid 3D net physics and responsive goal celebrations.

How to Play:

  • Aim and Strike: Click and hold to aim, release to shoot
  • Goalkeeper Save: Tap to dive in the direction of the ball
