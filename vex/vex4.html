Vex 4 - Fast-Paced Action Parkour Adventure

Vex 4 takes stickman parkour to the next level! Tackle 9 brand-new acts, challenge rooms, falling platforms, laser barriers, and zip lines while striving for gold trophy times.

Key Features:

  • Dynamic Level Mechanics: Master gravity portals, swinging ropes, water pools, and boost pads.
  • Challenge Room: Face 30 floors of continuous rapid-fire parkour trials.
  • Smooth Animations: Fluid stickman running, wall-sliding, and diving mechanics.
  • Checkpoint System: Touch green flag poles to save your progress throughout long acts.

How to Play:

  • Controls: WASD or Arrow Keys to move, jump, slide, and climb ropes.
  • Swinging Ropes: Press Left/Right to swing on ropes; press Up Arrow / W to jump off.
  • Swimming: Press Arrow Keys in water to swim in any direction.
