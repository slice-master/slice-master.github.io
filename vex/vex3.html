Vex 3 - Classic Stickman Parkour Platformer

Vex 3 is the iconic action parkour platformer! Guide your agile stickman through deadly obstacle courses filled with spinning saws, spike pits, ziplines, and falling blocks across 10 main acts.

Key Features:

  • Hardcore Parkour Platforming: Run, wall-bounce, slide, and swim through intricate obstacle courses.
  • 10 Challenge Acts & Hard Modes: Complete acts to unlock brutal trophy hard modes.
  • Interactive Level Hub: Explore the Stage Select hub map containing stage portals and secrets.
  • Trophies & Achievements: Earn gold medals for speedruns and flawless zero-death runs.

How to Play:

  • Move & Jump: Use WASD or Arrow Keys to run left/right and leap over hazards.
  • Wall Jump: Jump repeatedly against adjacent vertical walls to climb high shafts.
  • Slide & Crouch: Press S or Down Arrow key while running to slide under low spike ceilings.
  • Enter Stage: Stand on act blocks and press S / Down Arrow to enter levels.
