Vex 5 - Fifth Chapter Parkour Masterclass

Vex 5 brings all-new deadly traps and elevator mechanics! Navigate laser grids, saw blades, rocket boosters, and crushing blocks while unlocking trophy rooms and hard mode acts.

Key Features:

  • New Laser & Elevator Traps: Time your jumps past rotating laser beams and moving elevators.
  • Trophy Room Rewards: Collect stars and complete act objectives to unlock custom skins.
  • Brutal Hard Mode Acts: Re-play completed acts with extra spikes and faster trap speeds.
  • Crisp Controls & FX: Instant respawns and smooth 60fps platforming responsiveness.

How to Play:

  • Move Character: WASD or Arrow Keys for running, jumping, and sliding.
  • Slide Kick: Press S / Down Arrow while sprinting to slide through narrow low gaps.
  • Replay Checkpoint: Press R to immediately restart from your last active green checkpoint.
