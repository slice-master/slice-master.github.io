Funny Battle Simulator

Ragdoll Chaos and Tactical Combat

Funny Battle Simulator is a physics-based strategy battle simulation game. Strategically place your ragdoll warriors, knights, clubmen, and archers on the battlefield, manage your gold budget, and watch hilarious ragdoll physics unfold in glorious clashes!

Key Features:

  • Ragdoll Physics Engine: Hilarious unpredictable combat animations.
  • Diverse Unit Types: Knights, archers, spearmen, and colossal giants.
  • Tactical Strategy: Match unit counters and formations to defeat tough enemy waves.

How to Play:

  • Camera Pan: WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Rotate Camera: Hold Right Mouse Button and drag
  • Place Units: Left Click on grid
  • Remove Units: Right Click on unit
  • Start Battle: Spacebar or Start Button
