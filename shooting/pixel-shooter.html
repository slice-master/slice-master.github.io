Pixel Shooter

Retro 2D Platform Firefights

Pixel Shooter delivers fast 2D platforming gun battles in charming retro pixel graphics. Leap between floating platforms, grab heavy weapon crates, dodge incoming enemy fire, and blast your opponents off the map!

Key Features:

  • Retro 16-Bit Aesthetic: Crisp pixel sprites and explosive sound effects.
  • Chaotic Weapon Drops: Bazookas, laser rifles, shotguns, and machine guns.
  • Multiple Play Modes: Single-player campaigns and frenetic local multiplayer duels.

How to Play:

  • Move and Jump: A/D to move, W to jump
  • Shoot: Spacebar or Left Click
  • Switch Weapon: Q
